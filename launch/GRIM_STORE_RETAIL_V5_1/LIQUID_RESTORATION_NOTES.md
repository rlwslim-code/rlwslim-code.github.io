# GRIM liquid-glass restoration

The new `public/grim-liquid.css` and `public/grim-liquid.js` are loaded by `index.html`, `shop.html`, and `story.html`. They add a backdrop-blurred glass navigation bar, glass category labels, dynamic reflective cards and hero lens on hover-capable devices, and restrained ripple on buttons/cards when pressed. Mobile and reduced-motion support are built in. No commerce handlers changed.

The bundled original site did not contain an identifiable standalone liquid effect, so this implements a new visual treatment instead of claiming bit-for-bit recovery of prior live visual code.
